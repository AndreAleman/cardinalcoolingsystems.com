# Orders of $10,000 or more are paid by 50% deposit plus balance invoice

Under $10,000 an Order is paid in full by card, as the site works today. At or over $10,000 the buyer pays a 50% Deposit up front (card, or ACH via a Stripe invoice) and the remaining 50% goes on a Balance Invoice. We considered full-invoice / net terms and full card payment; the deposit gets half the cash in hand before Cardinal commits to sourcing, while keeping only one on-site payment flow. Net terms are deliberately out of scope.
