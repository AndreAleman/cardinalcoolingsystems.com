# Product photos: one Backdrop per store, 85% fill, square

Decided 2026-10-13 with the owner.

Product photos had drifted: most sat on a light-blue Firefly background, a
few on gray, and six pointed at dead links on the old sanitube.us and
BigCommerce sites, so the newest products showed red placeholder tiles at
the top of the Shop page. Products were also small in a wide 4:3 frame that
the square product tiles then cropped.

From now on:

- Every Product Photo is a cutout (Adobe Photoshop background removal) set
  on the store's Backdrop by a script, never regenerated by a generative
  tool, so every tile matches exactly.
- Frame: square 1600×1600, product's longest side at 85% of the frame —
  inside Google Merchant Center's 75–90% band — centered, on a floor with a
  soft contact shadow.
- Backdrop per store, chosen against the store's brand color so tiles
  stand out from the header and buttons without tinting the steel:
  - Cardinal (red brand): cool ice blue — red's complement, and it reads
    cold and clean for a cooling company.
  - Cowbird Depot (royal-blue/navy brand): cool slate gray — sits inside
    the navy scheme and stays distinct from Cardinal's blue. (Warm stone,
    the color-wheel complement, was rendered and passed over by the owner.)
  - SaniTube (cyan brand, gray logo): neutral steel gray.
- The main image is always a Product Photo. Lifestyle Photos go after it
  (Google wants the main image focused on the product). Drawings and 3-A
  marks stay in the gallery unchanged.
- Originals are kept, so changing a Backdrop later is a re-run, not a
  re-shoot.

Alternatives considered: pure white (Google's favorite, but the tile
merges into the white product card and white gaskets disappear); a dark
navy or deep-blue Backdrop (most striking, but black gaskets vanish and it
works against Google's preference for light main images); one shared
Backdrop for all three stores (the same products from three stores then
look identical side by side in Google Shopping).
