# Over-stock order lines can only be quoted, never bought

Cardinal sometimes fills large orders direct from overseas with lead times up to 10 weeks. We considered letting a buyer pay for more than we have on hand and warning them about the lead time. We decided instead that any Order Line whose quantity exceeds stock on hand (including zero stock) is a Quote-Only Line: it cannot be paid for on the site and must go through a Quote Request. This keeps Cardinal in control of pricing and lead-time promises on exactly the orders where both are uncertain, at the cost of a slower path for the buyer.
